# Watchlist Research

> **Physical role:** `docs/watchlist/development/research/` — experiment/hypothesis; draft mutable, locked research immutable.

Hypothesis, preregistration, candidate design, calibration, remediation, dan experiment records. Campaign ID seperti `C171` tetap dipertahankan bila memang identity riset.

## Recording / Mutability Rule

Research lifecycle:

`DRAFT -> LOCKED -> COMPLETED/CANCELLED`

- `DRAFT` boleh diperbaiki;
- setelah `LOCKED`, hypothesis/universe/threshold/gate/date split/acceptance tidak boleh diubah;
- perubahan membutuhkan research identity/version baru;
- hasil experiment masuk `../../records/evidence/`, bukan ditulis ke preregistration;
- research PASS tidak otomatis menjadi canonical strategy.

Universal rule: [`../../authority/governance/DOCUMENT_RECORDING_STANDARD.md`](../../authority/governance/DOCUMENT_RECORDING_STANDARD.md).

## Historical research boundary

This folder is for current research work. Completed/superseded legacy research from the original corpus is stored under `records/history/` with `LS-*`/`LX-*` provenance. Historical research may be consulted through the legacy indexes, but it is not current research authority.
