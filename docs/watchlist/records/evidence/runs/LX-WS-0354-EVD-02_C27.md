```text

      else R09 next-open-after-D1/D2/D3-close-profit rule

G16 = preplanned raw intraday target +1.50% else R09
```

All raw target prices are normalized to IDX tick bands. Close-signal exits remain next-open only; same-day close-signal exits are not allowed.
